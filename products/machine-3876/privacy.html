MACHINE 3876 Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CRUX HORIZON PTY LTD (“Crux Horizon”) handles information in connection with MACHINE 3876, a CRUX ARCADE game, and related support communications. It also describes advertising-related processing performed by Google when rewarded advertisements are shown.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

About Crux Horizon

MACHINE 3876 is published by CRUX HORIZON PTY LTD, an Australian software company. The public brand is Crux Horizon. MACHINE 3876 is part of the CRUX ARCADE series.

This product-specific policy supplements the Crux Horizon Privacy Policy. If there is a conflict for this game, this page applies to MACHINE 3876.

Overview

MACHINE 3876 is a portrait arcade colour-merge game. Gameplay runs on your device. Crux Horizon does not require a player account to play, and does not currently operate an online leaderboard for this game.

The game may display optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob so you can continue a run after Game Over. Advertising technology can process information even though Crux Horizon does not operate a game backend that receives your scores.

Accounts and Sign-In

MACHINE 3876 does not require a Crux Horizon account, username, or password. There is no Crux Horizon login used to play the game.

Local High Scores and Device Storage

The game stores a local top-10 high-score list on your device. Depending on how you play, that storage may include score values, wave progress, player initials you choose to enter, and related run identifiers needed to update the local board.

This high-score information is kept on your device for the game’s local leaderboard feature. Crux Horizon does not currently upload that leaderboard to Crux Horizon servers, and there is no Crux Horizon-operated online leaderboard for MACHINE 3876 at this time.

The game may also keep other limited on-device preferences (for example audio settings) so the experience works as expected between sessions.

Rewarded Advertisements (Google AdMob)

MACHINE 3876 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video advertisements. Watching an advertisement may unlock a continue that restores lives and resets the wave timer, according to the game’s rules. You can decline the advertisement and start a new run instead.

When ads are requested or shown, Google — not Crux Horizon’s own servers — may process information related to ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and (where permitted) advertising personalisation. Depending on platform, region, and your device settings, that processing can involve technical and advertising-related data such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, and interaction data with the advertisement.

Crux Horizon does not control Google’s full advertising systems. Google’s handling of information is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy materials, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent, Tracking, and Privacy Choices

The game integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) so that privacy and consent messages configured in Google AdMob can be presented where required. Whether a particular message appears can depend on your location, applicable law, AdMob messaging configuration, and prior choices.

On Apple platforms, apps that track users or access the advertising identifier for tracking-related purposes generally require permission through App Tracking Transparency (ATT). MACHINE 3876 includes the system usage description needed for that framework. Whether you see an ATT prompt, and how advertising behaves after you allow or deny tracking, depends on your device settings, operating-system version, and the advertising/consent configuration in effect.

Crux Horizon does not condition the core ability to play MACHINE 3876 on granting tracking permission. Rewarded continues depend on a successful advertisement experience where offered, not on agreeing to tracking.

You can also use device and account controls provided by Apple, Google, and other platforms to limit ad personalisation or reset advertising identifiers. See your device settings and Google’s privacy tools for current options.

Information Handled by Crux Horizon

Crux Horizon may handle:

  • information you voluntarily send by email or phone when requesting support or making a privacy enquiry;
  • business or project enquiry details if you contact us about other Crux Horizon services; and
  • limited technical information processed by our website hosting provider when you visit Crux Horizon web pages (see the company privacy policy).

Crux Horizon does not currently receive MACHINE 3876 high-score data from the game over a Crux Horizon game server, because local scores remain on the device.

Information Processed by Google

When the Google Mobile Ads / AdMob services run in MACHINE 3876, Google may process advertising and technical data as described in Google’s documentation and Google Privacy Policy. That processing is performed by Google as part of providing ads and related services. Exact data categories can change over time as Google updates its products; App Store and Play “data safety” / App Privacy disclosures for MACHINE 3876 should be read together with this policy.

Platform and Store Services

Apple, Google, device manufacturers, and app stores may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, update, restore, or back up the app, or when you use platform services. Crux Horizon does not control those independent services.

Retention and Deletion

Local high scores and on-device preferences remain on your device until you clear app data, reset the device, or uninstall the app (subject to how your operating system handles backups). Removing the app typically removes that local game data from the device.

Information you email to Crux Horizon is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, investigate issues, improve support, or meet legal, security, or record-keeping obligations. To request deletion of personal information you provided directly to Crux Horizon, contact us using the details below. We may retain limited information where retention is reasonably required.

For advertising data held by Google, use Google’s tools and policies, or contact Google where appropriate. Crux Horizon cannot directly delete data stored solely in Google’s advertising systems.

Children’s Privacy

MACHINE 3876 is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13. Crux Horizon does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through Crux Horizon support channels for this game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact support@cruxhorizon.com and we will take appropriate steps.

Parents and guardians should be aware that rewarded advertising uses third-party advertising technology. We do not intend to offer MACHINE 3876 in Apple’s Kids Category.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information Crux Horizon holds about you, or to make a privacy complaint. To exercise rights regarding information you sent directly to Crux Horizon, email support@cruxhorizon.com.

For choices about advertising and tracking on your device, use the privacy and advertising controls provided by your operating system and Google accounts, in addition to any consent interfaces presented by advertising messaging where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as MACHINE 3876, our practices, advertising partners, or legal requirements develop. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
