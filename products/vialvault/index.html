A Crux Horizon product

VialVault

VialVault is a privacy-focused inventory and scheduling platform designed to help users organise complex tracking workflows.

It brings inventory, schedules, and record history into one structured experience so personal tracking can remain clear and manageable.

Explore VialVault Return to Crux Horizon

Why VialVault exists

Clearer tracking starts with information in one place.

Personal tracking can become difficult when inventory details, schedules, and records are spread across disconnected tools or formats.

VialVault is being designed to provide a more organised workflow for maintaining information and understanding what has been recorded over time.

Core capabilities

Focused tools for structured personal tracking.

VialVault brings related tracking tasks together through a clear, consistent interface.

01

Inventory organisation

Structure and maintain inventory information in one organised place.

02

Scheduling and reminders

Create schedules and reminders that support a consistent tracking workflow.

03

Record history

Review recorded activity through a clear chronological history.

04

Compound library and search

Organise reference entries and find relevant information more efficiently.

05

Local privacy-focused storage

Keep primary application data stored locally on the user's device.

Privacy approach

Privacy is part of the product design.

VialVault is designed around user-focused data handling, with primary application data stored locally and unnecessary exposure kept to a minimum.
