Where software can help

Where software can make work simpler.

Not every friction point needs custom software. The useful first step is understanding the workflow and deciding what, if anything, should be built.

Spreadsheet-heavy work

Important processes live in shared spreadsheets that become fragile, hard to follow, or easy to break as the business grows.

Paper or manual processes

Handwritten forms, printed checklists, or verbal handovers make progress slow to track and easy to lose.

Duplicated data entry

The same information is typed into multiple places, creating extra work and inconsistent records.

Scattered information

Details sit across email, notes, folders, and tools, so people spend time searching instead of doing the work.

Repetitive administration

Routine steps take more time than they should and pull attention away from the work that actually needs your attention.

Field or mobile workflows

People away from a desk need a simple way to record, check, or complete work without cumbersome systems.

Software that almost fits

Off-the-shelf tools force workarounds because they do not match how the business actually operates.

An idea for a first version

You have a clear sense of a useful tool or product, and need a focused first version rather than a broad build.
