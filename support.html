CRUX ARCADE — MACHINE 3876

MACHINE 3876 is a neon arcade colour-merge game in the CRUX ARCADE series from Crux Horizon. Support for this title is available through the contacts on this page while the game is prepared for release and after it becomes available on the App Store.

MACHINE 3876 does not require a player account. High scores are stored locally on your device. Optional rewarded advertisements (Google AdMob) may be offered at Game Over so you can continue a run.

Rewarded advertisements

  • If an advertisement will not load, check your internet connection. VPNs or restricted networks can interfere with ad requests. Try again later, or choose ONE MORE? to start a new run without watching an ad.
  • If the game shows AD UNAVAILABLE, the continue reward was not granted. You can retry Watch Ad when connectivity improves, or start a new run.
  • If an advertisement appears stuck and will not close, leave the ad screen if possible, or force-quit and reopen the app. Do not assume a continue was granted unless gameplay resumes with restored lives.
  • Rewarded continues are optional. Core play does not require watching an advertisement.

Local high scores

  • The top-10 leaderboard is stored on your device only. There is no Crux Horizon online leaderboard for MACHINE 3876 at this time.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing app data typically removes local scores.
  • If initials or scores look wrong after an update, email support with your device details and we will help investigate.
