What this covers

The practical parts of putting a business website online.

The right scope depends on the business. A typical project is a responsive site plus the technical setup required for people to find it and contact you.

Responsive business websites

Pages that present the business clearly: what you do, where you operate, and how to reach you. Layouts are built to work on phones as well as larger screens.

New sites and replacements

A first website, or a replacement for an older one that is difficult to update, hard to read, or no longer represents the business.

Mobile optimisation

Text, navigation, and contact actions checked on small screens so the site remains usable when someone opens it on a phone.

Domain and DNS

Help registering a domain, or connecting one you already have, and setting DNS so the name points at the website.

Deployment and HTTPS

Publishing the site and configuring SSL so visitors reach it over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Contact and click-to-call

Email, a phone number, or another contact method that suits the business. A phone number can be a tap-to-call link on mobile.

Basic on-page SEO

Page titles, descriptions, headings, and indexing setup such as a sitemap, so search engines can find the site. This is setup of the site itself, not an advertising programme.

Hosting and maintenance

Ongoing hosting and routine care when you want the site looked after after launch. You can also take the site on yourself if that is the better fit.

How a website project runs

Start with what the site needs to do.

  1. 01
    Understand the business

    What you do, who needs to find you, and what should happen when someone gets in touch.

  2. 02
    Agree the scope

    Which pages are needed, whether this is a new site or a replacement, and what you can provide for the content.

  3. 03
    Build and publish

    Responsive pages, contact details, the domain connection, and HTTPS so the site can go online.

  4. 04
    Hand over

    You leave with a working site. Hosting and maintenance can continue with Crux Horizon when you want that.
