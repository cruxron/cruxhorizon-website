# Crux Horizon logo refinement exploration

These files are evaluation concepts only. They are not referenced by the live
website and do not replace the current production assets.

Each option contains:

- `horizontal.svg` — icon plus custom vector-path wordmark for dark surfaces
- `icon.svg` — compact colour icon on the dark brand tile
- `mono.svg` — single-colour transparent icon for one-ink and light-surface use
- `horizontal-mono.svg` — single-colour horizontal lockup for light surfaces

All artwork is SVG geometry with no text elements, fonts, raster images, or
external resources.

## Option 1 — CH monogram

A strong C enclosure intersects a blue H whose crossbar becomes the horizon.
The angled top of the H introduces direction without adding a separate arrow.

## Option 2 — Abstract horizon

A continuous forward path rises through a broad horizon band. The stepped
geometry creates a strong silhouette without dots or fine lines.

## Option 3 — Refined crux

Four weighted directional facets form a stable central crux. A restrained fifth
facet introduces a subtle Australian/Southern Cross reference without depicting
a constellation.

Evaluate icons at 16, 24, 32, 64, and 128 px. Do not select a concept from a
large presentation alone.

## Evaluation recommendation

Option 1 is the strongest candidate for refinement. At 32 px it retains the
clearest silhouette and the most ownable connection to the company name. The C
provides a stable enclosure while the H crossbar creates a horizon and its
angled top adds restrained forward direction.

Option 2 communicates progress immediately, but carries a higher risk of reading
as a generic growth arrow. Option 3 feels balanced and subtly references a
directional crux, but at small size it can resemble a familiar sparkle or
compass symbol.
